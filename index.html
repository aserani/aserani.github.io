Research programme

From simulation data to better design decisions

My work studies how geometry, physical information, and computational evidence can reveal lower-dimensional structure in difficult engineering design problems.

Design-space learning

Dimensionality reduction and design-manifold methods for understanding and navigating high-dimensional engineering geometries.

Physics-aware machine learning

Learning methods that incorporate physical information to support engineering analysis and optimization.

Multi-fidelity modelling

Surrogate and adaptive methods that combine computational models with different levels of cost and accuracy.

Simulation-based optimization

Methods for multidisciplinary engineering design, including computational fluid dynamics and uncertainty.

Methodological focus

Parametric Model Embedding
