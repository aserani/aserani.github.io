PME research line

Learning structure in parametric models

Andrea Serani is the lead developer of Parametric Model Embedding (PME). The research line develops from the original formulation toward representations that incorporate physical information and nonlinear design-space structure.

Connected themes

Methods for computational design

The programme spans methodological development and engineering application, with design decisions remaining the common focus.

Reduced design spaces

Methods for identifying compact representations of high-dimensional parametric shapes and engineering models.

Surrogates and multiple fidelities

Adaptive modelling strategies for combining evidence from simulations with different computational costs.

Uncertainty and robustness

Optimization methods for design problems affected by uncertain operating conditions and computational outputs.

Engineering applications

Computational fluid dynamics and multidisciplinary applications across marine, aeronautical, and structural design.
